# 当五个 AI 都同意我时，我是否更接近真理？

When Five AIs Agree with Me, Am I Closer to the Truth?

五个 AI 都赞同一个判断，究竟增加了几份证据？本文区分输出一致、事实正确、信念有据与主观确信，分析共识何时提高可靠性、何时只是重复同一个错误。

## 论文简介

文章采用十二项原始研究的定向综合、贝叶斯分析和精确概率构造，枚举五个二元系统的全部 32 种模式；没有开展新的模型或真人实验。

- 五个系统各有 80% 正确率，在均衡先验、对称且条件独立的设定下，全票一致时正确率为 99.90%；引入共同分量后可降至 88.63%，完全复制时为 80%。这些都是解析示例。
- 多数票正确率、一致条件下正确率和一致覆盖率是不同指标；成对相关性不能单独确定一致答案的可靠性。
- 保留独立初答、追踪共同来源、检查反例与允许弃权，可以使讨论回到可核查的理由；整套建议尚未作为组合验证效果。

数字不代表任何实际 AI 产品的可靠性。文献并非系统综述，部分仅核验原始摘要；二元对称模型不覆盖开放回答、价值判断和全部依赖结构。

两份 PDF 均含完整正文、方法、限制与参考文献。计算复核与 AI 辅助自查的范围在正文说明，不等同于外部学术审稿。

## English

### About the paper

When five AIs endorse a judgment, how much evidence has actually been added? The paper distinguishes agreement, factual correctness, justified belief, and subjective confidence, asking when consensus increases reliability and when it repeats a shared mistake.

**[Complete English PDF](en.pdf) · [Chinese PDF](zh.pdf) · [Discussion and feedback](https://github.com/lawyer112/20261026-five-ai-truth/issues)**

Author: lawyer112 · Paper ID: 20261026-five-ai-truth · Content version: 0.1.1 · Edition date: 28 October 2026

The analysis combines a targeted synthesis of twelve primary studies, Bayesian reasoning, and exact probability constructions enumerating all 32 patterns of five binary systems. It reports no new model or human experiment.

- With individual accuracy of 80%, balanced priors, symmetry, and conditional independence, accuracy conditional on unanimity is 99.90%. A common component can reduce it to 88.63%; complete copying leaves 80%. These are analytical examples.
- Majority accuracy, accuracy conditional on unanimity, and unanimity coverage differ. Pairwise correlation alone cannot determine unanimous-answer reliability.
- Independent initial answers, source tracing, counterexamples, and abstention help orient discussion toward checkable reasons; the proposed procedure has not been validated as a complete intervention.

The numbers do not estimate any actual AI product’s reliability. The literature selection is not systematic, some verification is limited to original abstracts, and a symmetric binary model does not cover open-ended answers, value judgments, or every dependence structure.

Both PDFs contain the complete text, methods, limitations, and references. The paper explains the scope of computational verification and AI-assisted self-checks; these are not external academic review.
